2. What This DGP Stresses (Intent)

Purpose: Penalize estimators that rely on L2 loss under heavy-tailed noise.

This DGP is identical to the baseline linear design except for the outcome noise, which is a Gaussian/Cauchy mixture. The Cauchy component makes the population mean of observed outcomes undefined, so any estimator that minimizes squared error is theoretically ill-posed and will suffer variance explosion.

This motivates quantile-based estimators (e.g., GenGC targeting medians) that are well-defined under heavy tails.

3. Identification Assumptions (Explicit)

  • Unconfoundedness holds by construction (treatment is assigned by a known propensity score).
  • Overlap is moderate (p(X) bounded away from 0 and 1 for most of the mass).
  • Structural ATT is defined as the mean of the signal tau(X) on treated units (Constitution Article I), independent of realized noise.

4. Mathematical Specification

Covariates

\[ X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_5) \]

Propensity

\[ p(X) = \text{expit}(0.5 X_1 - 0.5 X_2) \]

Baseline Outcome and Treatment Effect

\[ \mu_0(X) = 1 + X_1 + 0.5 X_2 \] \[ \tau(X) = 1 + 0.5 X_1 \]

Noise Mixture (Heavy Tails)

\[ \varepsilon \sim 0.8 \cdot \mathcal{N}(0, 0.5) + 0.2 \cdot \text{Cauchy}(0, 1) \]

Moment note: The Cauchy component has undefined mean and variance. Therefore the population mean of realized outcomes is undefined, and any L2/MSE estimator is not well-posed in the population.

Potential Outcomes

\[ Y_0 = \mu_0(X) + \varepsilon_0,\quad Y_1 = \mu_0(X) + \tau(X) + \varepsilon_1 \]

5. Oracle Truth Definition

  • ATT (Structural): \(\mathbb{E}[\tau(X) \mid W=1]\), computed from the deterministic signal \(\tau(X)\). This is well-defined even when \(\mathbb{E}[Y]\) is not.
  • QST (Distributional): computed on the canonical tau grid via oracle Monte Carlo as specified in cs_get_oracle_qst().

7. Empirical Validation

We run light validation with n = 1000 and seeds = 1:20. The goal is to show variance explosion for OLS under L2 loss. We do not run GenGC here to keep doc builds fast.

Empirical validation (n=1000, seeds=1:20)

lm_att -2.301 9.184
oracle_att 0.000 0.000

Interpretation: OLS fails not because it is biased in the limit, but because its variance is infinite under a Cauchy mixture. The RMSE will fluctuate wildly across seeds (typical values ~8.0 in quick runs). The oracle estimator remains stable, confirming the truth layer.

8. Failure Mode Summary

  • L2-based estimators (OLS/IPW with mean targets): variance explosion; unstable RMSE.
  • Quantile-based estimators: well-defined under heavy tails (motivation for GenGC).
