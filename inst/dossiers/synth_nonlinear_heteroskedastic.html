2. What This DGP Stresses (Intent)

Monotone nonlinear confounding + “black swan” heteroskedastic noise. OLS fails because it fits a plane to a cubic signal and cannot adapt to variance that explodes in the tails of the covariate distribution.

3. Identification Assumptions (Explicit)

  • Selection on observables holds.
  • Overlap is moderate.
  • SUTVA holds.

4. Mathematical Specification

Outcome (control): \[ \mu_0(X) = 1 + 0.5 X_1^3 + 1.5 X_2^2 - 1.0 X_4 \]

Propensity: \[ p(X) = \mathrm{expit}(1.5 X_1 - 1.5 X_2) \]

Heteroskedastic noise: \[ \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}\!\left(0, \sigma^2(X)\right),\quad \sigma(X) = 0.1 + 1.0\exp(0.5 X_2) \]

7. Empirical Validation

OLS (lm_att) fails in v1.4.0 benchmarks (Bias > 0.4, RMSE > 1.0): the cubic confounding drags the linear fit, and exponential variance produces “black swan” realizations that dominate squared loss. Oracle passes by construction.

8. Failure Mode Summary

  • Linear models underfit nonlinear structural signal (bias).
  • Exponentially varying noise creates outlier-driven variance and destabilizes uncertainty quantification.
